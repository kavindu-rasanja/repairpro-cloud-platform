# Data Protection and Privacy

## Data categories

RepairPRO may process:
- customer names and contact numbers;
- device descriptions and identifiers;
- repair descriptions, photos or notes if enabled;
- staff identities and access records;
- sales, payments and inventory records;
- SMS delivery metadata.

Collect only information necessary for the business workflow. Avoid collecting national identity numbers or unrelated sensitive details unless a documented legal and operational need exists.

## Principles

- Provide a clear privacy notice to each business customer.
- Define the business's role and RepairPRO's role in processing.
- Limit access by tenant, branch and job responsibility.
- Encrypt data in transit and protect local data at rest.
- Define retention periods for customer, ticket, payment and audit records.
- Provide a controlled export and deletion process where legally appropriate.
- Keep production data out of development, screenshots and issue reports.
- Document subprocessors, hosting regions and SMS providers.
- Obtain appropriate consent or other lawful basis for customer SMS messages.
- Provide opt-out handling where required by law and provider policy.

## Sri Lanka launch review

Before launch, obtain qualified advice on applicable Sri Lankan privacy and data-protection requirements, including the Personal Data Protection Act and current commencement / amendment status. Do not claim legal compliance until counsel has reviewed the actual data flows, contracts, notices, hosting and operational controls.
